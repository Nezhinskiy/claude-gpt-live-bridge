Branch `docs/0.4.0-sweep` at `8af785e`, on PR 42's `9507b77`. Reviewed `git diff 9507b77...8af785e`:
`README.md`, the six translations, `SECURITY.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `site/index.html`,

## Important

**D-1. "One request in five" is the old rule's figure.** `README.md:466-467`, the translations (read
in ru and fr), and `docs/adr/0001-platform-findings.md:153`. The sentence says about one in
five "in testing, with the voice's rule against it in place". In `docs/bugs/BR-001.md` that rate is
rule A's: 3 of 20, and 3 of 10 in the probe. The rule in the code is C (`54ecab1`), measured at
0 of 20, which BR-001 says cannot be told from luck and bounds below about 14 %. The sentence
predates the sweep, but it is the first limit a reader sees and the only figure in this diff that
does not match its source. It overstates the rate for today's rule, while "with the rule in place"
reads as today's. BR-001 also warns that one English request in a synthetic voice is all that was
measured. Suggested: "about one in five with the rule as first written, and 0 of 20 with the rule
now, too few to tell from luck".

**D-2. A data path sync added is not named.** `SECURITY.md:217-218`. The sweep rightly adds that
text typed into a running turn sits in the mod's own store, under the pause too. The same store now
holds the whole `tool_input` of every open permission prompt (`plugin/hooks/turns.ts:153`, the
`prompts` field of the snapshot): a Write's content, or a command with a secret in it, in plain JSON
until the prompt settles. `SECURITY.md` and the README's Privacy section say nothing of it, and
BR-002 lists it only as later work on a hash. The session's transcript holds the same input, so the
risk is small, but "every data path" is the claim this section exists to make. Also, "that store is
not in the data folder" does not say where it is. On this Mac it is
`~/.claude/plugins/store/livebridge_livebridge-….json`. Name the path, the tool input, and that
`/live off` and a session's end delete the key. Whether `claude plugin uninstall` removes the file
was not run, and the README's Uninstall should say if it does not.

## Minor

**D-3. "Only" is too strong on the site.** `site/index.html:124`: the line "opens only when someone
speaks or when Claude answers you". A question and an approval notice open it too, and an approval
notice is not an answer. Since this sentence is about what is billed, drop "only" or use the
README's list (`README.md:343-345`).

**D-4. The headline does not say that typing goes to OpenAI.** `README.md:37-42`,
`site/index.html:185`. Sync is on by default, and "Local by design" names only "the voice line".
Nothing there is false, and `README.md:403` says it plainly, but this is the largest new thing that
leaves the Mac. Site line 193 says "nothing leaves your Mac until a line opens" without saying what
leaves then. One clause in each: "and what you type in a joined session, once a line opens".

**D-5. The site omits the first limit.** `site/index.html`. It carries the silent reopened line, the
notice the voice may not say and the usage limit. It does not carry BR-001, the voice saying it is
looking with no handoff, or the sound that tells them apart; nor the desktop origin. Its one link to
the known limitations is on the managed-settings clause. A sentence, or a plain link to the limits,
is enough.

**D-6. The desktop origin is findable, but late.** `README.md:507`, the eighth limit of 15, while
line 32 says flatly that the voice follows what you type. It is the limit most likely to hit a
reader of this README who uses the desktop app, and it ends in "the menu says **Context up to
date**" with text missing. Move it up, next to the voice's limits.

**D-7. A garbled sentence.** `README.md:334`: "In another session it goes as a result from it
would". The translations read correctly, so only the English needs fixing.

**D-8. One string kept the old term.** `CodexLaunch.swift:71` (`CodexProblem.hint`) still says
"realtime voice". The research note says so, row 4, and leaves it. It takes one word.

**D-9. Translations: one word for three things.** `README.ru.md` uses "запрос" for a voice request,
a typed prompt and a permission prompt; `README.fr.md` does the same with "demande". English keeps
them apart, and it matters here:

- "На линии, которую открыл запрос" (ru, `:498`) and "une ligne qu'une demande a ouverte" (fr) read
  as a voice request. The source means a permission prompt. Use "запрос разрешения" and "demande de
  permission".
- "Вопрос решает один запрос" (ru, the desktop limit) reads oddly in a section where "вопрос" is
  Claude's question.

Otherwise both are faithful, figures included. I found no change of meaning in the passages read.

**D-10. Long lines.** `README.md:41,234,380` and `CHANGELOG.md:18-19,112` run past 100 columns.
`docs/research/2026-11-05-gpt-live-limits.md`'s quoted table keeps repo-root links, which do not
resolve from `docs/research/`. Both are cosmetic.

## The checks asked for

No, but for D-1 and D-3. Checked and correct at `9507b77`:

| Claim | Against |
|---|---|

| 5 s on a reopened line; 20 s; 90 s; 2 minutes | `firstWordSeconds`, `silenceSeconds`, `noiseCapSeconds`, `resultWindowSeconds` |
| 64 lines, 20 sessions, 128 items, 8,192 tokens, 2,000 and 1,500 characters, 80-character title | `keptLines`, `keptConversations`, `maxItems`, `maxTokens`, `maxCharacters`, `maxContext`, `maxTitle` |

worst text at the limit takes about 1.2 s.

### Are the real limits honest and findable?

Yes, in the README. BR-001 is first, the voice not saying a notice second, the silent reopened line
fifth with its figure, and the desktop origin is stated with what it costs (D-6 on its place). The
bullet that sells walking away (`README.md:308-314`) ends with "do not rely on that when away from
the Mac". `SECURITY.md` says the notice is "no control and no promise". That
is the right balance: "its answer opens the closed line again and goes to the voice to say" is
confident and true, and the limit is one bullet down. The site is honest on two of them (D-5).

### Is `SECURITY.md` exact?

Exact on the pause, and better than the code's own comments. Every promise and non-promise I tested
holds: nothing typed is recorded, the mark, no summary by itself, the waiting request with why it
differs from a later one, the title, a non-`composer` prompt, a turn with no `start`, `/live
context`, a forget. The data paths are all named but one (D-2). The new "a local process can open
the billed line" bullet is right, and its condition, a turn of yours in the conversation, is one
such a process can post itself.

### Anchors
